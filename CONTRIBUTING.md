# Contributing to Frosty Zugbonk

Bug reports, ideas, and feature requests are welcome through GitHub Issues.

For now, please **do not submit code, graphics, or other copyrightable material
in pull requests without prior written agreement with Zugbonk**. The project
is offered for free noncommercial use while commercial licensing rights remain
reserved. Outside contributions need appropriate permissions for the project
to offer those commercial licenses later.

You may maintain and share your own noncommercial modifications under the
terms of [LICENSE](LICENSE), including the required attribution in [NOTICE](NOTICE).

Project website: https://www.zugbonk.com
